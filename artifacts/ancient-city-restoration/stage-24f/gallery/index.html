How these images were made. Every image is rendered from the actual restored NBT block states with the vanilla 26.1.2 client's block models and textures (stairs, slabs, walls, doors, beds, lanterns, water, glass and orientation are real). Lighting is flat studio shading — not in-game lighting — and beds/chests/skulls are rebuilt from entity textures. These are not Minecraft-client screenshots. The blocky figure beside entrances is a 1.8-block scale reference. Tap an image to enlarge.

Priority design questions
