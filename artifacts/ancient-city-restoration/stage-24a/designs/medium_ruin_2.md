## Size conflict and alternatives

With original Y=5, floor at y=0 leaves only y=1..3 below roof y=4. A second floor needs its own floor plus at least two blocks of usable clearance, and stairs need headroom. Fitting a genuine partial upper story within those five layers would reduce one or both floors to an unusable one-block-high space or remove the roof. Therefore the requested partial second floor is **BLOCKED at the original size**.

1. **Preferred role-preserving option:** approve Y=8 only (X/Z stay 16×11; connector remains at source coordinates) and use the two-level concept above.
2. **Strict size-preserving alternative:** keep 16×5×11 and redesign as a one-story split-level home with a raised sleeping dais/mezzanine platform and 3-block room, then revise the role from “partial second floor” to “split-level one-story.” It is not a true second floor.

No jigsaw metadata or coordinate changes are authorized by either concept. No footprint or height has been changed in this package.
