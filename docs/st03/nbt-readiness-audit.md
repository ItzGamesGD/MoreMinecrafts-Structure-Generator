# ST-03 NBT readiness audit (2026-10-30)

Verdict: **NOT READY**. Source: ST03_PHASE_B2_V2_AUDIT_20261029.zip SHA-256 `0b025a524fbbf8ffd3ad590babebe108327c79f90f603765a86d1e0d4b17ea5b`, all packaged checksums verified. This is a design audit; no production templates were built.

- The package's section 7 and `modules.py`/`assembly.py` define 25 module footprints, sizes, 34 placements, rotation, air mode and order. These are placement specifications, not a complete three-dimensional block-by-block source for all 25 modules. `details.py` explicitly describes plans/elevations, with ambiguous legend entries such as stairs/slabs and material mixes. Section 19.3 R3 explicitly says the tower spiral block geometry is unproven. Exact positions, IDs, and states for every layer, roofs/corners, openings and stair runs must be authored from the drawings and reviewed; they cannot be asserted as already specified. In particular the owner/designer must approve or provide a traversable tower spiral and exact facing/half/shape wherever drawings do not determine them.
- `decor.py` supplies 216 coordinate/state entries in 17 modules; that does not define the complete shells, interior volumes or all surrounding states. Resolve any collisions, support and air semantics once the complete block grids exist. Preserve authored stair states per ST-03-STAIR-Q01; compute permitted wall/bar connections against those states. Verify the final grids and actual assembly in all four rotations.
- Section 18.2 and `be.py` specify empty chest/barrel and furnace/blast furnace entities and wall-banner patterns, without world coordinates in entity NBT; container fill is deferred to runtime. Confirm placement/state and payload against the actual grids and BE-01. Standing banners lack evidence and are excluded. Loot assignment remains a separate implementation decision.
- Ground modules use air=include and walls air=omit; section 8 defines foundation/terrain policies and the adaptive gate approach. SEAM-01 treats unknown exterior or omitted-air neighbours as air, so actual terrain-facing connections and later updates need a specified integration and validation strategy. Terrain thresholds are proposed, and the three review biomes need owner decisions for worldgen; those are distinct from authoring static templates.
- Visual validation: package section 10 specifies views V01–V24 and records version, commit, camera, rotation and elevation. Its D9 precheck launched an actual 26.3 client on a separate test platform, using Fabric client gametest, Xvfb and Mesa lavapipe Vulkan; it is not an ST-03 image. After templates exist, load the assembled structure in an isolated client world, capture those views including day/night and boundary, inspect spiral traversal and record visual failures as VISUAL BLOCKED if the client cannot render. Owner visual review remains necessary.

The existing SEAM-01 live proof uses synthetic content; real ST-03 assembly, stair preservation under the final placement path, and external boundaries are not yet verified. The MMB worldgen hook is excluded from this task. Do not infer missing geometry or start building production NBT until the designer supplies or approves the missing block-level details.
